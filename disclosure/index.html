    Affiliate Disclosure

    Updated 1 June 2026

    Movelli is free to read and carries no display advertising. We fund the site through affiliate partnerships. This page explains exactly what that means.

    What an affiliate link is

    Some outbound links to providers (such as eSIM apps, VPNs and banks) are affiliate links. If you sign up or buy through one, the provider may pay us a commission. You pay exactly the same price — often less, when we can pass on a deal.

    How it affects what we recommend

    It doesn’t. We recommend products on merit. We’re happy to feature providers that don’t pay us a cent, and we’ll tell you when a free option beats a paid one. If a recommendation ever reads like an ad, we’ve failed — please tell us.

    How you can spot them

    Affiliate links are marked up with rel="sponsored" and typically open in a new tab. We don’t hide them, and you’re never required to use them — going directly to a provider works just as well.

    A note for the FTC

    In line with US Federal Trade Commission guidance, we disclose that Movelli has material affiliate relationships with some of the providers mentioned across this site.
